Split Keymap

One keymap for two split boards: the wireless Aurora Lily58 (ZMK) and the wired Keebio Iris rev7 (QMK). BASE, NAV, SYM and FN are identical on the main block; only the thumb row differs because the Iris has three thumb keys per half instead of four. The OS stays on the Norwegian layout on every host.

Where the shell characters live

  • / . , ; ' on BASE; Shift gives _ : * for free on the Norwegian layout. The Lily58 also has = and - on its inner keys.
  • SYM right home row, the best spots: " on H, - on J, _ on K, = on L. Å Ø Æ stacked in the column after that (P, ;, /), ? on the . key.
  • SYM left hand: bracket pairs on resting finger pairs: ( ) on A S, { } on D F, [ ] on Z X. | on G, \ on C, & * on V B, ~ < > $ % on the top row.
  • The rare € and ^ are pushed to the outermost right column; ` on Y, + on N.

Arrows on the left hand

NAV is held by the left thumb and the same hand steers: S left, D right, F up, V down, so the right hand can stay on the mouse. Home and End on A and G bracket the arrows, PgUp above the up arrow on R, PgDn beside the down arrow on B, word-jump under left/right on X and C, word-delete on W and E. Shift and Ctrl on the left pinky column pass through, so select-word and jump-word need no extra layer.

Thumb rows

Lily58 (4 per half): GUI, Alt, NAV, Space / Enter, SYM, Backspace, FN. Inner row-4 keys carry = and -.

Iris (3 per half): GUI, NAV, Space / Enter, SYM, FN. Alt moves to the left inner row-4 key where it was before; the right inner key gets -, the most typed shell symbol. The extra thumb Backspace is gone; Backspace is top-right as on a normal keyboard.

Switching hosts (Lily58 only)

Hold FN and press 1 to 5. Each key is a ZMK macro: &bt BT_SEL n then &rgb_ug RGB_COLOR_HSB(hue,100,20), so the underglow colour says which host is active: red, green, blue, yellow, purple. The Iris is wired, so its FN top row controls RGB instead.

macOS

Profile 1 is the Mac. Selecting it turns on the MAC layer, a flag that leaves BASE alone (the GUI thumb is Cmd and ALT is Opt on macOS natively, Ctrl stays on the pinky) and makes NAV + MAC automatically become the Cmd/Opt variant of NAV: Option word jumps, Cmd zoom, Cmd same-app window switching. On the Iris, FN + MAC toggles the same thing by hand.

Still open: the Norwegian Mac layout puts @ $ brackets | \ on other Option combos, so a Mac SYM layer is the next step.

Lily58 hardware overlay

config/splitkb_aurora_lily58.overlay moves the nice!view chip-select to P0.08, because the Aurora uses P0.06 (the adapter's default CS) for the WS2812 data line, and sets the LED chain to 34 per half (5 underglow + 29 per-key). Without it the display and LEDs fight over one pin.

Firmware

Lily58: ZMK, config/, locale header keys_nb.h, flashed as .uf2 onto the NICENANO drive. Iris rev7: QMK, qmk/keebio_iris/, keymap_norwegian.h, flashed as .hex with QMK Toolbox (ATmega32U4, Atmel DFU bootloader). Both from napahlm/split-keymap.
